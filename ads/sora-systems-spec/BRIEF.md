## 6. Music (original, made in code)

- **Key/tempo:** E♭ minor at **120 BPM**. This is deliberately not the reference's D minor; we take the gesture, not the notes.
- **Bars 1–2 (0–4 s):** sub drone with an ember-crackle texture, and a tonal riser into the flash at 1.00.
- **Bars 2–4 (3–8 s):** **clock-tick pulse** (filtered 16th-note ticks), glassy arpeggio, soft kick on beats 1 and 3. This is the night-shift motor.
- **Bar 5 (8–10 s):** the arp opens up (filter sweep) as the clock hits 07:00, then a stack/check hit.
- **Bar 6 (10–12 s):** a riser builds and **cuts to near-silence at 11.7**.
- **Bar 7 (12.0):** **lift to E♭ major.** Wide pad, sub drop and shimmer on the horizon hit.
- **Bars 8–9 (14–18 s):** the brand chord holds under the tagline, then it resolves on the end card.
- **No VO, so no ducking.** The mix is built like the reference: mono-leaning centre, with width only on the pads and the horizon hit.
- **Master:** −14 LUFS integrated / −1 dBTP (true-peak measured at 4× oversampling).
